# SLSFC Digital Design System — Guidelines

> SLSFC Digital Design System **v1.0** — implementation snapshot.
> Authoring source: the hosted SLSFC Digital Design System (https://claude.ai/artifact/XJgvEM7VTUzrAbjbswy9fK), version `1790961796-c84a`, approved 2026-10-02.
> Asset paths are localized to this repo; wording is otherwise identical to the hosted guidelines.
> Do not edit this file to change the design system. Change the hosted system first, then re-sync. See `brand/README.md`.

St. Louis Squirrel Football Club (SLSFC) — the Squirrels — is a youth soccer club in Webster Groves, Missouri. The identity is **modern, athletic, confident and community-oriented** — a club, not a cartoon. The squirrel lives in the crest; the rest of the system speaks in navy, orange, condensed type and clean structure. Values: **Community. Character. Confidence.**

## Source of truth

- The **master crest** (`brand/logos/slsfc-crest.svg`, from `SLSFC_Crest_Master_RGB.ai`, kept as `brand/logos/slsfc-crest-master-rgb.ai`) is authoritative for the crest and for the core color values. Where a board differs, the master wins.
- The identity and kit boards (held in the hosted design system, not in this repo) set the broader language: tracked-caps captions, the short orange accent bar, navy bands, the collar trim. They are reference images, never production art.

## Voice and copy

Write for parents first, players second. Plain, warm, direct; short declarative sentences. The boards set the cadence: fragments with full stops — "Bold. Confident. Ours." "Clean. Classic. Always a Squirrel."

- Use **we** for the club and **you / your player** for families. "Your player gets a team, a coach and a place to grow."
- Headlines: short statements, uppercase via the display styles.
- Restraint over rules. We're a community youth club, so we can be playful or celebratory now and then — a season opener, a tournament win, a thank-you to volunteers — without sounding juvenile, gimmicky or mascot-driven.
  - **Exclamation marks:** occasional, for real celebration or welcome; one at a time. Not in navigation, buttons, form errors, schedule changes or cancellations.
  - **Wordplay:** light and rare, and it should still read clearly without the joke. The squirrel is not a running gag — no steady stream of nut, tree or acorn puns, no cartoon voice.
  - **Emoji:** sparingly, in social posts and informal team or family messages. Not in UI labels, headings, buttons or official notices.
- Labels and buttons: verbs, uppercase via `label`. "Register", "View schedule", "Volunteer".
- Age groups as **U8**, ranges as **U9–U10** (en dash). Times as **9:00 AM**. Scores ours first: **3–1**.
- Naming:
  - **Formal first reference:** St. Louis Squirrel Football Club. Never "St. Louis Squirrel FC".
  - **Subsequent references:** SLSFC or the Squirrels.
  - **Team-facing / brand expression** (kits, headers, hero lockups, chants): SQUIRRELS. In running text, "Squirrels" or "the Squirrels".
- Say the hard things plainly: "Saturday's U10 games are cancelled. Fields are closed for rain."

## Color

- Lead with `navy` and `orange`; together they are the brand. `white` is the third partner, `slate` a quiet support.
- `navy` (#042644) and `orange` (#FC4B08) are the master-crest values. They replace the boards' #082A4A / #08244A and #F15A24 everywhere.
- `orange-deep` (#C93008) is shading inside the crest artwork, not a general brand color. Its one approved UI use is as the light-theme value of `accent-text`, the label of the orange soft `Tag` on `accent-soft` (4.8:1). Never a fill, never a second brand orange, never extended to other text.
- Build pages from the semantic tokens, not the brand primitives: `surface`, `surface-alt`, `surface-raised`, `band`, `ink`, `ink-muted`, `accent`, `action`, `on-action`, `line`, `line-strong`.
- **Light theme = away kit** (white ground, navy type). **Dark theme = home kit** (navy ground, white type). Both share the same orange.
- Body-size text is navy family, not orange. Eyebrows use `ink-muted`; links, ghost actions and nav use `ink`. Orange arrives through non-text devices beside them — the accent bar, a link's underline, the active-nav bar — so hierarchy holds without orange text.
- `accent-text` is used in one place: the label of the orange `Tag`, on `accent-soft` (4.8:1 light, 6.1:1 dark). Don't extend it to eyebrows, links or navigation.
- Brand `orange` as text is for display sizes (24px+, such as the ProgramCard age numeral) and marks only — it is 3.4:1 on white.
- Links in running text: `ink`, underlined, with the underline in `accent` (`.sq-link`); on hover the underline thickens. Underlines stay visible so links never depend on color.
- Primary buttons: `action` fill with `on-action` (navy-deep) label, 5.2:1. Never white on orange.
- Full-bleed `band` sections (hero, values, footer) carry `on-band` text, `on-band-muted` secondary text and orange accents.
- Status — `success`, `caution`, `danger` with their `-soft` grounds — always carries a word ("On", "Weather watch", "Cancelled"). `danger` is a crimson set well apart from brand orange; never use brand orange to mean an error.
- Keep orange to roughly a tenth of any screen: accents, the one primary action, the age numerals. Navy and white do the rest.

## Typography

- **Approved.** Barlow Condensed and Barlow are the SLSFC digital typography system, approved for v1.0. Use them for all web and digital work.
- Two Google families: **Barlow Condensed** (`display`) for the athletic voice, **Barlow** (`sans`) for reading and labels. Load: `Barlow 400/500/600/700`, `Barlow Condensed 600/700/800 + italic 700/800`.
- `display-xl` and `display-lg` are italic 800, uppercase — the energy of the SQUIRRELS kit lettering. Use at most one per page.
- `heading-1` to `heading-3` are upright 700, uppercase. `score` is for numerals with tabular figures.
- `body-lg`, `body`, `body-sm` are sentence case. Keep lines to 60–72 characters.
- `label` and `eyebrow` are the boards' wide-tracked captions; always uppercase. Eyebrows sit above an accent bar and a title: `eyebrow` (in `ink-muted`) → `accent-bar` → `heading-2`.
- Barlow Condensed is not the SQUIRRELS wordmark and never substitutes for it. Until the custom wordmark artwork exists, display type may set the word for headline energy only; do not slant, stretch, outline or customize type to imitate the kit lettering. Once the wordmark exists, use the artwork wherever the SQUIRRELS mark appears, and never Barlow Condensed in its place.
- Under 640px drop display sizes one step (80→56, 56→40, 40→32).

## Layout and spacing

- 4px base: `space-1` … `space-24`. Components use `space-2` to `space-6`; sections use `space-16` (mobile) and `space-24` (desktop).
- Content width `container-max` (1200px); gutters `space-4` on phones, `space-8` on desktop. 12-column grid, `space-6` gutters.
- Alternate sections `surface` / `surface-alt`, and break long pages with one navy `band`. Structure comes from color blocks and lines, not floating cards on grey.
- Mobile first: every component works at 360px. Touch targets are at least `control-height` (44px).

## Shape, lines and depth

- Square-shouldered, like the shield: `radius-none` for bands and bleeds, `radius-sm` (2px) for buttons, tags and inputs, `radius-md` (4px) for cards. No pills, no large rounding.
- **Accent bar** — `accent-bar-width` × `accent-bar-height` (40 × 4px) in `accent` — is the system's signature. Put it under eyebrows and at the top edge of cards. One per block.
- **Trim stripe** — three `trim-stripe` bands (orange / white / navy) from the kit collar — divides a band from the page edge or tops the footer. Use it sparingly: once or twice per page.
- `line` for hairlines, `line-strong` for any border that defines a control. Shadows are low and navy-tinted: `shadow-sm` at rest, `shadow-md` on hover and for menus.
- Focus: a 2px gap in the surface color, then a solid 2px `focus-ring` (orange). On bands, the gap is `band`.

## Logo and crest

- The crest (`slsfc-crest.svg`) is the club's primary mark. Use the file as supplied — never redraw, recolor, crop the shield or separate the squirrel from it.
- Place it on `white`, `paper` or `navy`. On photography, only over a calm, dark area. Never on orange.
- Minimum on-screen height `crest-min` (32px); below that, set the name in type. Clear space on all sides is at least a quarter of the crest's height.
- Raster needs (email, social, favicon fallback): `slsfc-crest-1024.png`, `slsfc-crest-256.png`. Print and vendors: the master, `slsfc-crest-master-rgb.ai`.
- The boards also show a round squirrel mark, a tail-only secondary mark, shield variants (B clean, C mono) and a Gateway Arch mark. None of these has production artwork yet — do not recreate them from the boards.

## Imagery

- Real players and families in Webster Groves, shot in natural light. Kits visible, faces joyful but not posed.
- Crop tight and let photos bleed to the edge (`radius-none`) or sit in `radius-md` frames. A navy overlay (navy at 60–70%) is allowed only to carry white text.
- Photograph minors only with documented guardian consent; no full names with faces.
- No clip-art squirrels, cartoon acorns or stock trophies.

## Iconography

- No icon set has been chosen yet. Until one is, use simple 2px-stroke line icons with square caps, in `ink` or `accent`, at 20 or 24px — and always alongside a word.
- The squirrel is not an icon. Do not use it as a bullet, loader or decoration.

## Motion

- Quick and direct: 150ms ease for color and shadow changes, 200–250ms for menus. No bounces, no mascots running across the screen. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
