## Display (display)

APPROVED (v1.0) — Barlow Condensed (Google Fonts), the SLSFC display face. The condensed, heavy, athletic voice. Italic 800 for the biggest moments; headings upright 700; uppercase. Not the SQUIRRELS wordmark: never used to imitate it, and never in its place once the wordmark artwork exists.

| Style | Size / line | Weight | Style | Tracking | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `display-xl` | 80px / 0.92 | 800 | italic | -0.01em | One per page: the home hero and season launches. Uppercase. Drops to 56px under 640px wide. The sample is set type, not the SQUIRRELS wordmark. |
| `display-lg` | 56px / 0.95 | 800 | italic | -0.005em | Landing-page heroes and campaign headers. Uppercase. 40px under 640px. |
| `heading-1` | 40px / 44px | 700 | normal | 0.01em | Page titles. Uppercase. 32px under 640px. |
| `heading-2` | 30px / 34px | 700 | normal | 0.01em | Section titles, under an eyebrow. Uppercase. |
| `heading-3` | 22px / 26px | 700 | normal | 0.02em | Card titles and sub-sections. Uppercase. |
| `score` | 48px / 1 | 700 | normal | 0 | Scores, jersey numbers, key figures. Use tabular numerals (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums). |

## Text (sans)

APPROVED (v1.0) — Barlow (Google Fonts), the SLSFC text face. Pairs with the boards' wide-tracked captions. Sentence case for reading; tracked uppercase for labels.

| Style | Size / line | Weight | Style | Tracking | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `body-lg` | 19px / 30px | 400 | normal | 0 | Lead paragraphs under a hero or section title. |
| `body` | 16px / 26px | 400 | normal | 0 | Default reading text. Sentence case. Measure 60–72 characters. |
| `body-sm` | 14px / 22px | 400 | normal | 0 | Supporting text, card meta, form help. |
| `label` | 13px / 16px | 600 | normal | 0.14em | Buttons, tags, nav links, table headers. Always uppercase. |
| `eyebrow` | 12px / 16px | 600 | normal | 0.24em | The wide-tracked caption from the boards: above section titles, captions on imagery. Uppercase, in ink-muted (on-band-muted on band), with the accent bar carrying the orange. |

## Responsive

Under 640px wide, drop display sizes one step: 80→56, 56→40, 40→32.
